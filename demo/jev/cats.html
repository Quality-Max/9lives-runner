What makes the second room tricky?

Some of those pointy ears belong to cushions! Look for stitched seams, little fabric tags and X-shaped button eyes. The living cartoon cats have sleepy curved eyes and whiskers.

The two rooms use the same drawing and hiding places. Three animals become cushions in the trickier room. The answer map is part of this pretend game.

Use Tab to move between hiding spots and Enter or Space to check one. You can also reveal numbered hiding places and read their list below.

    Let Jev play too

    You can play and reveal the cats without a key. To send the picture to Jev, connect TypeSafe in the terminal, then reload this page.

    read -rs 'TYPESAFE_API_KEY?TypeSafe API key: '
export TYPESAFE_API_KEY
npm run demo:jev -- --live --images
unset TYPESAFE_API_KEY

    One click sends one clean synthetic room picture with three questions: are there cats, how many, and where is one? Your taps, answer map, room name and correct count stay out of the model’s input. Up to six live clicks per local session, shared with the notebook demo.

    Images and Box are experimental TypeSafe features. This is a game to explore their behavior; live accuracy has not been qualified. A guess never clicks the picture or adds to your score.
